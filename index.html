    How it works

    Three steps, no account and no install.

    1. Allow the microphone

      Your browser asks once. You can change this any time in site settings.

    2. Record your take

      Press the pink button to start and again to stop. The timer shows the length.

    3. Play or download

      Listen back straight away, then save the file to your device.

    What you get

    A small tool that does one job and stays out of the way.

    • Private by design

      Audio never leaves your device. There is no server and no tracking.

    • Live waveform

      See your voice as you speak, so you know the microphone is working.

    • Works on any device

      Phone, tablet or laptop, in current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.

    • Keep several takes

      Record as many times as you like and download only the ones you want.

    Questions

    Quick answers. Select a question to open it.

    Where are my recordings stored?

    Only in your browser’s memory while the page is open. Nothing is uploaded. Download a take to keep it, because refreshing the page clears the list.

    Why does the browser ask for microphone access?

    Recording needs the microphone. The permission is requested only when you press the record button for the first time.

    The microphone is blocked. How do I fix it?

    Click the lock or settings icon next to the address bar, set Microphone to Allow, then reload the page and try again.

    What file format do I get?

    It depends on your browser. Chrome, Edge and Firefox save WebM or OGG, and Safari saves M4A. All of them play in most players, including VLC.

    Is there a recording length limit?

    The page sets no limit. Long takes use more memory, so download and delete older ones if you record for a long time.
